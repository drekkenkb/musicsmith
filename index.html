MusicSmith

  1. Open a ROM. Every song (and sound effect) is listed, ready to play.
  2. Export them as MIDI files and edit them in any music app, or edit the notes right here.
  3. Import the edited MIDI files back.
  4. Save the ROM, or an IPS patch you can share.

Each game stores its music its own way, so MusicSmith has to know the game's sound engine. It knows:

    What you hear is the game's own sound engine running, so it's what the game will play.

    Your ROM never leaves this device. Your work is kept in this browser until you close the ROM.
